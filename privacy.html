Privacy Policy: gta_shorts

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Who we are

gta_shorts is a private tool operated by one individual (the "operator") to create and publish short videos to the operator's own social media accounts. It has no public users. Contact: potatodragonincorporated@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

gta_shorts uses YouTube API Services. By using content or features made available through gta_shorts that rely on YouTube, you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What data we access

  • Public YouTube data through the YouTube Data API: titles, durations, view counts, language, channel names and upload lists of public videos, plus the text and like counts of public comments (used only to find timestamps that viewers mention).
  • The operator's own YouTube account, through OAuth with the scopes youtube.upload (to upload approved videos to the operator's own channel) and yt-analytics.readonly (to read that channel's performance reports).
  • The operator's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account, to publish approved videos.
  • We do not access any other person's account, and we do not collect personal data from viewers.

How we use it

  • To find notable public gameplay moments and to credit the original creator on screen and in the video description.
  • To upload videos the operator has manually approved to the operator's own accounts.
  • To learn which video styles perform best on the operator's own channel.

How we store it, and for how long

Data is stored only on the operator's private server.

  • YouTube search results: 6 hours.
  • Comment text and timestamps: 24 hours.
  • Video metadata used to find moments: up to 7 days.
  • Nothing obtained from the YouTube API is kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed or deleted. The one exception is a usage record of the video IDs and time ranges we have excerpted, which is kept so we can credit creators and avoid reusing the same clip.
  • The operator's own analytics reports are kept for the operator's use.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the server and are never shared.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or share YouTube API data with anyone. To build videos, short excerpts and the related public text (such as titles or comment snippets) are processed by Google's Gemini API (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Finished videos are sent to the operator's private Telegram chat for review. Videos are only published to the operator's own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Cookies and devices

gta_shorts does not place cookies on, or collect information from, any visitor's browser or device. This website itself does not use cookies or analytics.

Revoking access

The operator, or any account holder who has connected an account, can revoke gta_shorts' access to Google and YouTube data at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Access to Meta and TikTok can be revoked in those services' app settings.

Deleting data

To request deletion of any data relating to you or your content, for example if you are a creator whose public video was excerpted and you want it removed from our usage record, email potatodragonincorporated@gmail.com. We delete it within 7 days. Creators can also ask for any published clip of their content to be taken down at the same address. See also the data deletion instructions.

Changes

We will update this page if our use of data changes and show the new date.

Facebook, Instagram and TikTok

The app holds OAuth access and refresh tokens for the operator's own Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and TikTok account. They are used only to publish videos the operator has approved. The permissions requested are:

  • Facebook: post to the operator's Page and read the Page's basic info.
  • Instagram: publish content to the operator's account.
  • TikTok: upload and publish videos, plus basic profile info.

To find trending videos, the app also reads public metadata, such as titles, view counts and public clips, from the Reddit and Twitch APIs. Tokens are not shared, not sold and not used for advertising, and are kept until the operator revokes them. Generated videos are deleted automatically when storage fills up. The app does not collect data from anyone other than the operator.

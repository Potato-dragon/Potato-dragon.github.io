Top5 Shorts

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Top5 Shorts is operated by the Top5 Shorts operator ("the operator") as a personal tool. This policy explains what data it uses and how.

Data the app accesses

The app accesses only the operator's own accounts. It holds OAuth access and refresh tokens for the operator's YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and TikTok account. These tokens are used only to upload videos the operator has approved. The permissions requested are:

  • YouTube: upload videos.
  • Facebook: post to the operator's Page and read the Page's basic info.
  • Instagram: publish content to the operator's account.
  • TikTok: upload and publish videos, plus basic profile info.

Public data

To find trending videos, the app reads public metadata, such as titles, view counts and public clips, from the YouTube Data API and the Reddit and Twitch APIs.

How data is stored and shared

Tokens are stored only on the operator's private server. They are not shared, not sold and not used for advertising.

Other people's data

The app does not collect data from anyone other than the operator. This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Retention

Tokens are kept until the operator revokes them. Generated videos are deleted automatically when storage fills up.

YouTube API Services

Top5 Shorts uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

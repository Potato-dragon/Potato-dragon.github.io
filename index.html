Top5 Shorts

Top5 Shorts is a personal tool that makes short "Top 5" gaming highlight videos with original commentary and posts them to its operator's own accounts: a YouTube channel, the Facebook Page "GTAVI clips", the Instagram account @gt_a_a_a_vi and a TikTok account. Every video is reviewed and approved by the operator before it is posted. It is not a service for the public, and it has no users other than its operator.

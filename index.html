gta_shorts

gta_shorts is a private, single-user tool that the owner of the "GTAVI clips" channels uses to make short gaming videos about Grand Theft Auto VI. It finds notable moments in public gameplay videos, makes short vertical clips with original captions and credits the original creators. After the owner manually reviews and approves each video, it posts it to the owner's own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

Top5 Shorts

Data deletion instructions

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Top5 Shorts only stores OAuth tokens for the operator's own accounts. To remove the app's access, revoke it on the platform:

  • Google / YouTube: go to Google security settings and remove the app.
  • Facebook: Settings → Business integrations, then remove the app.
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites, then remove the app.
  • TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions, then remove the app.

Tokens stop working immediately after you revoke them, and they are then deleted from the server.

You can also ask for deletion through the contact method.
